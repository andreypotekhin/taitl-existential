## Large validation-only workflows

Each sample used 10,000 entities, 200 descriptors, and 100 validation declarations, with one warmup and three measured
workflows. Setup/finalization was outside timing. Reporting, commit, and complete workflow durations are separated.
Every process completed with 12,000,000 expected validation callbacks across warmup and measured work. The rejection
workflow explicitly rolled back; transactions and the library instance closed. Values are microseconds and allocated

and recovery ran exactly 10,000 callbacks at commit with none during reporting. Every immediate workflow ran exactly
